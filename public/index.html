What WebGrade actually does

01

Renders the real page

Opens your live site in a real browser at desktop and mobile viewports. No source-code guessing.

02

Captures screenshots, runs Lighthouse, and extracts structural signals from the rendered result.

03

Analyzes visual quality

AI inspects hierarchy, typography, color, spacing, responsiveness, and common AI-builder patterns.

04

Delivers an actionable report

Clear scores, concrete strengths, prioritized improvements, and estimated opportunities.
